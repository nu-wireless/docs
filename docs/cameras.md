# Security Camera Use Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of a security camera system is to deter and investigate unauthorized
access, theft from or damage to the Wireless Club lab, while protecting the
privacy of club members and guests.

Any camera installation is to be done for security purposes and is not to be
used for any purpose unrelated to incident investigation.

## Installation

In order to protect the privacy of club members, camera installations are
limited to capturing entry and exit points on the club space. Hidden or covert
cameras are not allowed; any camera must be reasonably visible to the average
observer.

Audio recording, facial recognition, motion tracking, or any other biometric or
automated identification feature must be disabled prior to installation. Cameras
which do not allow such features to be disabled shall not be installed.

Any camera installation, relocation, or modification which goes beyond the scope
of maintenance requires:

- A majority vote of the Officer Board at a Board Meeting.
- A 14-day prior notice to general club membership.

## Footage Access

Footage captured on camera may only be accessed during an incident
investigation. General observation is strictly prohibited. An Incident Report
must be started prior to camera access.

Accessing the camera footage must be done at a Board Meeting with at least 3
Officer Board members present. An access log entry must be completed for every
review of the footage performed.

*Live monitoring of camera feeds is prohibited* (may be potentially needed for
investigation?)

Any access to the camera footage server should be automatically logged to an
append-only log and trigger an alert.

*Credentials to the camera system must be made avaiable to the Officers.*
(common credentials or on a person basis?)

## Retention, Copying, and Disclosure

Footage shall be kept for no longer than 31 days on a local system in a locked
enclosure. Cloud storage and third-party access shall be avoided to the extent
that is possible.

While viewing the footage, the Officer Board may vote to copy footage relevant
to the incident. The footage may be used as evidence to resolve an internal
incident and parts included in the incident report.

No footage may be copied, exported, photographed, screen-recorded, or shared
outside without a board vote recorded inside the Access Log.

Camera footage may be shown to the parties involved in an incident, if the Board
finds it appropriate. No public sharing of any camera footage on social media or
otherwise is permitted.

Camera footage may be disclosed upon request to Northeastern University
officials and law enforcement.

## Access Log

An Access Log shall be kept in club documents recording every access to the
camera system performed. An entry in the Access Log must contain the following
entries:

- Incident report name
- Board Meeting date
- Board Meeting roster
- Time ranges viewed
- Description of footage copied
- Outcome of the review

The Access Log is to be available for inspection by any member upon request.

## Maintenance

When the Board finds necessary through a vote, a time frame may be designated
for maintenance and testing of the camera system. A 3 day prior notice to
general membership is required before the maintenance period can take effect.

The Officer Board may access footage recorded during the maintenance period for
the purposes of camera maintenance or testing the system out. Footage recorded
during the maintenance period may be accessed without an ongoing investigation,
for the duration of the maintenance period.

An Access Log entry must still be filed for every access of the footage.
